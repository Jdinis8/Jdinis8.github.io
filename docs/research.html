Research Interests

Status: Active / May 2026

My research interests spread throughout several areas of General Relativity and Cosmology. Currently, I am working in Post-Newtonian theory, modelling rotating thin shells of matter, investigating Relativistic Positioning Systems, exploring phenomenological studies of radiation in general, and Numerical Relativity. My Master's Degree thesis was on hyperboloidal slicing, which involved a higher focus in Numerical Relativity. I have also worked on applications of machine learning for parameter estimation.

I am also very interested in the origins of science, and the scientific method. A remarkable example that I have found was the work of Abu al-Ala al-Ma'arri (973 – 1057), the Syrian philosopher and poet. His extensive text, the Epistle of Forgiveness, that predates the Divine Comedy in its rendering of a journey to Hell, has a very interesting quote:

"Space is that in whose smallest division all the time can happen and time is that in whose smallest division all space is contained."
